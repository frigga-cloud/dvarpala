Allow a domain

The part after the @. Matching is exact, so frigga.cloud does not admit mail.frigga.cloud — add that separately.

A whole address works too — the domain is taken from it.

A person whose domain is not listed sees exactly the same “check your email” page as everyone else, and no code is sent. That is deliberate — the portal must not become a way to discover who works here — but it means a wrongly missing domain looks like mail not arriving. The audit trail is where it shows up.
